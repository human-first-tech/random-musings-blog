# Random Musings — Writer Guide

Everything you need to write, publish, and manage your blog. No code, no tech — just Notion.

---

## Writing & Publishing a Post

1. Open the **Writings** database in Notion
2. Click **+ New** to create a new row
3. Fill in these fields:
   - **Title** — the headline of your post
   - **Slug** — the URL of your post. Use lowercase letters and hyphens only, no spaces or capitals. Example: `my-cup-of-chai` ✅ — `My Cup of Chai` ❌
   - **Excerpt** — 1–2 sentences that describe the post. This appears on cards and as the pull-quote at the top of the article.
   - **Category** — pick one from the list
   - **Publish Date** — the date to display on the post
4. Click into the row to write the body of your post
5. When you're ready to go live, set **Status → Published**
6. The site updates within 60 seconds ✅

> **Draft tip:** Leave Status as **Draft** while you're writing. The post won't appear on the site until you change it to Published.

---

## After Publishing — Did the Subscriber Email Go Out?

When you publish a post, an email is automatically sent to everyone who has subscribed. Here's how to confirm it went out:

1. Open the **Writings** database in Notion
2. Find the post you just published
3. Check the **Notified** column — if it shows a ✅ checkbox, the email was sent successfully

That's it. You don't need to do anything else — it's all automatic.

## Checking Comments

When a reader leaves a comment on any post, you'll receive an email notification with the reader's name and comment, and a link to review it in Notion.

To see all pending comments:
1. Open the **Comments** database in Notion
2. You'll see all comments with their status: **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**
3. The **Post Slug** column tells you which post the comment is on

---

## Approving a Comment

1. Open the **Comments** database
2. Find the comment you want to approve
3. Click the **Status** field → select **Approved**
4. The comment will appear on the site within 60 seconds ✅

---

## Rejecting a Comment

If a comment is inappropriate or you simply don't want to publish it:

1. Open the **Comments** database
2. Find the comment
3. Click the **Status** field → select **Rejected**
4. The comment will not appear on the site (and if it was previously approved, it will be removed within 60 seconds)

---

## Deleting a Comment

If you want to permanently remove a comment from Notion:

1. Open the **Comments** database
2. Find the comment row
3. Right-click the row → **Delete**
4. The comment will be removed from the site within 60 seconds

## Slug Rules (Important)

The slug is the URL of your post. A bad slug will break the link. Always use:

- ✅ Lowercase letters only
- ✅ Hyphens to separate words
- ✅ No spaces, no capitals, no apostrophes, no special characters

| Good | Bad |
|---|---|
| `my-cup-of-chai` | `My Cup of Chai` |
| `the-art-of-saying-no` | `The Art of Saying No!` |
| `letter-to-my-younger-self` | `letter to my younger self` |
