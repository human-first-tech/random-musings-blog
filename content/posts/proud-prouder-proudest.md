"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams" — Eleanor Roosevelt

This post of mine is dedicated to few people in my life who are a living epitome of the quote by Roosevelt. Every time I talk to them or talk about them I feel a sense of pride showcasing their journey and achievements. I can brag about them forever and I am always so amazed and inspired by each of them.

It is a small list but a growing one and I would like to thank them in my own way for just being fab and wish them lots of luck as they continue in their journey.

This is my version of their journey — of changes, risks taken by them, how they challenged status quo, their hard work and their passion to do something. Their actual story of their life is theirs to tell and being the modest people they are they would never divulge all the great things they have done or continue to do.

There is something incredibly humbling about watching the people you love grow into the best versions of themselves. It makes you want to be better too. It makes you want to chase your own dreams with the same reckless abandon.

The people in my life who inspire me most are not the ones who had the easiest paths. They are the ones who stumbled, fell, got back up, and kept going. They are the ones who chose the unconventional path when the conventional one seemed safer. They are the ones who said — I am going to do this my way.

To my tribe of dreamers, doers, and quiet revolutionaries — this one is for you. You make me proud every single day. And because of you, I believe a little more in the beauty of my own dreams.
